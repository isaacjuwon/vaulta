# About Vaulta

**Vaulta** is your own personal two-factor authenticator. It gives you a safe, private place to keep the verification codes that protect your online accounts.

---

## What is Two-Factor Authentication?

When you log into an account like your email, bank, or social media, entering just a password isn't always enough to keep you safe. Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of security by requiring a unique 6-digit verification code that changes every 30 seconds. 

Even if someone guesses or steals your password, they cannot access your account without that code.

---

## Why Choose Vaulta?

Most people use authenticator apps made by big tech corporations. While convenient, those apps often store your security keys on corporate cloud servers, tie your security to their platforms, and can lock you out if you lose access to their services.

Vaulta does things differently:

- **You Are in Control**: Your codes are stored under your own roof on your personal instance. No third-party corporation has access to your keys.
- **Complete Privacy**: Vaulta does not track you, does not include advertisements, and never collects data about what services you use.
- **Works Everywhere**: Access your codes from any device with a web browser — whether you are on your smartphone, tablet, laptop, or work desktop.
- **Works Offline**: Once installed on your phone or computer, Vaulta generates your verification codes seamlessly, even if you are on an airplane or have no internet connection.
- **Never Locked In**: You can export and backup all your accounts at any time. If you ever want to switch to another app, you can take all your codes with you with one click.

---

## How It Works

Using Vaulta is simple and straightforward:

1. **Add an Account**: Turn on two-factor authentication on any service (like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, or your bank). 
2. **Scan or Paste**: Point your camera at the setup QR code or copy the setup link.
3. **Get Your Code**: Vaulta generates your 6-digit security code instantly and refreshes it every 30 seconds.
